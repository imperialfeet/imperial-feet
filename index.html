Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does toenail fungus treatment take?+

Toenails grow very slowly: a full nail can take around a year to replace itself, so antifungal routines need months of daily consistency before the healthy nail fully grows out. Stopping early is the most common reason treatments appear to fail. Explore the nail fungus treatment range to build a routine you can stick with.

Why should I disinfect my shoes during treatment?+

Fungal spores survive in warm, damp shoes and socks, so freshly treated nails can easily get reinfected from your own footwear. The Imperial Feet Shoe & Sock Spray is made to disinfect shoes and socks alongside the nail treatment, breaking that cycle.

What does the Nail & Sole Foot Soak do?+

It is a pedicure-strength soak with tea tree oil and epsom salt. Regular soaking softens hard skin and thickened nails, which helps topical treatments penetrate better, while improving the appearance of yellow toenails and refreshing tired, itchy feet.

Can I use these products for dry, cracked heels too?+

Yes. Alongside the antifungal range, Imperial Feet makes daily-care products like Foot Balm Blue with glycerin, rosemary and pine extracts for dry and cracked feet. If you have diabetes or circulation problems, check with your doctor before starting any new foot-care routine.

Where are Imperial Feet products made?+

The range is developed in the Netherlands and manufactured in the Netherlands and the USA. The brand has been family-run since 2001 and its professional formulas are used by pedicurists and pharmacies in more than 20 countries.
